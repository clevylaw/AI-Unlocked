Learn to use AI well in legal work

Short lessons in plain English for law students, lawyers, paralegals and anyone who works in law. You don’t need any technical skills.

Who it’s for

Law students, lawyers, paralegals, legal staff and legal technologists. Maybe you have never used a chatbot. Maybe you use one every day and want to use it safely with client work. The lessons start simple, use real legal examples and build up to your professional duties.

How it works

  1. Answer 4 short questionsWe suggest the lessons that fit you, in order.
  2. Read or listen to each lessonEach one takes about 5 minutes.
  3. Try it, then check yourselfTry the idea in a real AI tool, then answer one question.

What you’ll learn

  1. 1What AI isWhat artificial intelligence is, how it learns, and where lawyers already meet it.
  2. 2Generative AIAI that drafts, sums up and makes pictures and voices. What it means for evidence, copyright and fraud.
  3. 3Chatbots and AI modelsHow chatbots work, how to ask good legal questions, why they invent cases, and how to pick a tool.
  4. 4AI in legal workUsing AI for legal writing, for law school and at a legal job, within the rules.
  5. 5Fairness, privacy and legal jobsBias in AI used for bail, sentencing and hiring, what happens to what you type, and how legal jobs are changing.
  6. 6What’s next in AIAI agents that carry out tasks, AI that reads scans and transcribes speech, and how to keep up with the rules.
  7. 7How AI works insideAn extra module for technical readers and legal technologists. Covers tokens, training, how legal AI tools are built, agents and testing.
  8. 8Your professional dutiesHow the ethics rules apply to AI: competence, confidentiality and privilege, courts and candor, and checking AI research.
  9. 9Legal AI tools and AI lawThe main kinds of legal AI tools, how to choose one, and the laws that regulate AI.

Built on primary sources

Lessons cite the documents lawyers rely on, and each lesson lists its sources. Examples include:

  • ABA Comm. on Ethics & Pro. Resp., Formal Op. 512, Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools (2024)

  • United States v. Heppner, No. 25-cr-503 (S.D.N.Y. Feb. 17, 2026)
  • N.D. Tex. Local Civil Rule 7.2(f), disclosure of generative AI use
  • Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the Artificial Intelligence Act
