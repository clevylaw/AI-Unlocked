Module 6 review

Review: What’s next in AI

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • AI agents carry out tasks with many steps, so your duty to supervise covers their work.
  • AI can read scans and transcribe speech. Check recording laws and treat transcripts as drafts.
  • Keep up by knowing your bar’s guidance, your courts’ rules and your employer’s policy.
