Module 4 review

Review: AI in legal work

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • Let AI write a first draft, then check every fact and legal point and rewrite it in your own words.
  • In law school, follow your school’s rules and use AI to test yourself, not to skip the reading.
  • At work, use only approved tools for client information, bill honestly and supervise others’ AI use.
