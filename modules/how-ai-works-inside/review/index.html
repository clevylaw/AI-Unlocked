Module 7 review

Review: How AI works inside

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • Models read and write tokens, only see what fits in the context window, and pick each token by chance.
  • Training gives a model knowledge up to a cutoff date. Feedback training makes it helpful, and sometimes too eager to answer.
  • Most legal AI tools combine a model with search over real legal sources. Test them on your own kind of work.
