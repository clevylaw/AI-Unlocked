Module 7 review

Review: How AI works inside

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • Models read and write tokens. They only see what fits in the context window, and they pick each token by chance from a list of likely ones.
  • Pretraining gives a model its knowledge. Later training on example chats and feedback makes it a helpful assistant, and training on checkable problems teaches it to reason.
  • Most AI products combine a prompt, looked-up information and tools. Build a set of tests and run them after every change.
