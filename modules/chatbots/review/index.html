Review: Chatbots and AI models

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • A language model writes by guessing the next word, over and over.
  • Clear, detailed prompts with some background get much better answers.
  • Chatbots can state wrong facts and sound sure, so check anything important.
  • Different models suit different jobs. Large ones are for hard problems, and small ones are for quick tasks.
