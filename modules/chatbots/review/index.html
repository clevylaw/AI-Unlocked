Module 3 review

Review: Chatbots and AI models

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • A language model writes by guessing the next word, so it can produce cases that sound real but are not.
  • Good prompts give your role, the place, the task and the format, and leave client details out.
  • Lawyers have been sanctioned for filing fake AI citations. Read every case you cite.
  • Legal research tools are better than general chatbots for finding authority, but they still make mistakes.
