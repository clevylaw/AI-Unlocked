Module 5 review

Review: Fairness, privacy and legal jobs

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • AI can repeat unfair patterns. Courts and lawmakers have set limits on tools like risk scores and hiring screens.
  • AI chats may be saved, read or handed over. A court has held a client’s own AI chats were not privileged.
  • Routine review and drafting are changing most. Judgment, advocacy and client care matter more.
