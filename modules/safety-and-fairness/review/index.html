Review: Fairness, privacy and jobs

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • AI can repeat unfair patterns from its training data. This matters most when AI helps make decisions about people.
  • Never share passwords, ID numbers or other people's private information with a chatbot. Check your privacy settings.
  • AI is changing everyday computer tasks the most. Work that needs judgment, care and creativity is changing the least.
