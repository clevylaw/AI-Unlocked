Review: What AI is

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • AI is software that learns patterns from examples. Nobody writes all its rules by hand.
  • Courts have accepted some AI for years, such as technology-assisted review in e-discovery.
  • AI has no duties and cannot be disciplined. The lawyer stays responsible for the work.
