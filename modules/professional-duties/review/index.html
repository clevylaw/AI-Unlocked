Module 8 review

Review: Your professional duties

A short recap of the module, then one question from each lesson. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • ABA Formal Opinion 512 and state bar guidance apply the existing ethics rules to AI. Check your own state's rules.
  • Get informed consent when required before putting client information into an AI tool, and read the tool's terms.
  • Check the court's local rules and the judge's standing orders on AI before every filing.
  • Find, read and check every authority yourself. Never ask the AI to check itself.
