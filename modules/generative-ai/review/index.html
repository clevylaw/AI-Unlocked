Review: Generative AI

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • Generative AI makes new text, pictures, sound and video. A fast draft is only useful once someone checks it.
  • AI can make fake photos, voices and videos that look real. Ask where any file came from before relying on it.
  • Courts are still deciding who owns AI-made work and what AI may train on. Confirm payment changes by calling a number you already have.
