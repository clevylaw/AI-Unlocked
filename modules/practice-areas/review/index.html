Module 10 review

Review: AI by practice area

A short recap of the module, a question from each lesson and a scenario to apply what you learned. Answering again later helps you remember.

What you learned

  • Litigators can use AI for review, summaries and drafts, but must check every citation and quote and follow court rules on AI.
  • Transactional lawyers can speed up contract review, but should check a sample by hand and decide the hard calls themselves.
  • In-house teams use AI and also set the company’s AI rules, including for high-risk uses such as hiring.

Review quiz

One question from each lesson, with the answers in a new order, then a real-world scenario. If you miss one, you get a second try.

Question 1 of 3

A colleague used AI to sum up a 300-page deposition. What should happen before the summary is used in a brief?

Question 2 of 3

An AI tool reviewed 200 supplier contracts and listed every change-of-control clause. What is the right next step?

Question 3 of 3

The marketing team wants to use a new AI tool to screen job applicants. Why should the legal team review it first?

Scenario

You want to load documents produced under a protective order into an AI review tool. What do you check first?
