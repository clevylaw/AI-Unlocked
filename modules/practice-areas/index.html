AI by practice area

How AI shows up in litigation and discovery, in contracts and deals, and on in-house legal teams.

3 lessons, about 15 minutes

Start this module →

Lessons in this module

  1. 1AI in litigation and discoveryLitigators, the lawyers who handle lawsuits, use AI to review documents, sum up testimony and draft. Each use comes with its own risks and rules.5 min
  2. 2AI in contracts and dealsTransactional lawyers, who handle contracts and deals, use AI to review contracts, compare versions and check drafts against their preferred terms. Speed is the benefit, and missed terms are the risk.5 min
  3. 3AI for in-house teams and legal operationsIn-house lawyers, who work for one company, and legal operations teams often decide which AI tools a company uses and write the rules for using them.5 min
