From static to a picture

Most picture tools use a method called diffusion. In training, the AI takes real pictures and adds random dots, like the static on an old TV. It keeps adding dots until nothing is left but static. Then it practices the opposite. It turns static back into a clear picture, a little at a time.

Think of it this way

Picture a sandcastle washing away in the waves, one frame at a time. Now imagine watching that film millions of times. In the end, you could play it backwards and build a castle out of loose sand. Picture AI learned to build pictures out of static in a similar way.

Where your words come in

When you type a description, another AI turns your words into directions. The picture AI then cleans up the static step by step. At each step, it moves closer to a picture that matches your words. The result is new, but it is shaped by the millions of pictures the AI studied.

A different picture every time

Each try starts from different random static. So the same words give you a different picture each time. That helps when you are exploring ideas. Make a few versions and keep the one you like best.

Tip for describing a picture

Say what is in the picture. Then add the style, the light and the mood. "A quiet waterfall in a thick Japanese forest, early morning light, watercolor style" gets a much better result than "a waterfall."
