AI that makes things

Older AI mostly checked things. It answered questions like "Is this email junk?" or "Is this photo a cat?" Generative AI makes things. You describe what you want, and it creates something new.

Key idea

Generative AI creates new content. It does this using patterns it learned from a huge amount of content made by people.

What it can make

Text
Emails, stories, summaries and translations. Examples are ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.
Pictures
Photos, drawings and logos made from a description. Examples are Midjourney, Ideogram, Adobe Firefly, and the picture tools inside ChatGPT and Gemini.
Sound
Realistic voices and whole songs. Examples are ElevenLabs and Suno.
Video
Short clips made from a description or a photo. Examples are Google Veo, OpenAI Sora, Runway and Kling.
Code
Working computer programs from requests in plain English. Examples are GitHub Copilot, Cursor and Claude Code.

Why it matters

It used to take years of training to make good writing, design, music or software. Now anyone can make a first draft of almost anything in seconds. Skill and good taste still matter a lot. Generative AI gives people a quick place to start.
