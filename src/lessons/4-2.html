Explanations that fit you

Tell the AI what you already know and what confuses you. It will change its explanation to fit you.

A good learning prompt

"I'm new to investing. I know what stocks and bonds are. I don't understand the difference between an index fund and an ETF. Explain it simply, and compare it to something from everyday life."

Teach it back

You understand something well when you can explain it simply. So explain a topic to the AI in your own words. Then ask it to point out mistakes or anything you left out. This is called "teach it back," and it is one of the best ways to learn.

Practice questions

Ask the AI to quiz you. For example, "Give me 5 multiple-choice questions about the French Revolution at a high school level. Ask them one at a time and tell me if I'm right." When you get one wrong, ask the AI to explain the answer.

Study tools made for this

Some tools are made for learning from your own notes and files. One example is Google's Gemini Notebook, which was called NotebookLM until mid-2026. It answers questions using only the documents you upload. It can also turn them into a spoken summary you can listen to.

For important subjects, check facts in a textbook or on a website you trust. AI helps you learn faster. You still need reliable sources.
