How fast things change

A few years ago, AI could barely write a paragraph. Now it drafts documents, holds spoken conversations and reviews contracts. New models come out every few months. Courts, bars and lawmakers are writing new rules about AI at the same time.

A simple routine

  • Know the rules that apply to you. Check your bar's ethics guidance, the rules of the courts you appear in and your employer's or school's AI policy.
  • Follow one or two trusted sources. Examples are the ABA Journal, your state bar's news and a legal technology newsletter you trust.
  • Use the tools. Ten minutes trying a feature on a made-up problem teaches more than an hour of reading.
  • Come back to this site. The AI tools and AI models pages are updated every week.

A healthy way to think about it

Nobody knows everything about AI. You understand the main ideas, the main risks and your duties. If you keep practicing with approved tools and checking their work, you will keep up.
