How fast things change

A few years ago, AI could barely write a paragraph. Now it can write software, hold spoken conversations and make realistic video. New models come out every few months. What seems amazing today will seem normal next year.

A simple routine

  • Pick one or two sources and check them once a week. Examples are MIT Technology Review, the AI section of The Verge, or a newsletter you trust.
  • Use the tools. Ten minutes trying a new feature teaches you more than an hour of reading about it.
  • Come back to this site. The AI tools and AI models pages are updated every week.

A healthy way to think about it

Nobody knows everything about AI. You understand the main ideas from this course. If you keep practicing with the tools that matter to you, you will keep up.
