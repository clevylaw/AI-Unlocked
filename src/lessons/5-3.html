What we know so far

Experts do not agree on how big the change will be. Some think many jobs will disappear. Others think AI will change most jobs and create new ones, as past inventions did. Almost all of them agree that many jobs are already changing.

What history shows

The printing press, factory machines and the internet each ended some jobs and created others. The change was often hard on the people who lost their jobs. New jobs did not always appear quickly, or in the same places.

Jobs AI changes most and least

AI is changing everyday computer tasks the most. These include typing in data, simple writing, basic number work, some customer service and translation.

It is changing hands-on skilled work the least, such as the work of plumbers and electricians. Caring work, like nursing, is changing less too. So is work that depends on judgment, trust and leading people.

How to get ready

  • Get used to AI tools by using them for parts of your own job.
  • Build skills that AI finds hard, such as judgment, working with people, deep knowledge of your field and creativity.
  • Keep learning. A course like this one is a good start.
