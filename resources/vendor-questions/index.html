Questions to ask an AI vendor

Questions to ask before a firm, court, school or legal department adopts an AI tool.

· Report a correction (opens GitHub in a new tab)

AI for Legal Work · https://clevylaw.github.io/AI-Unlocked/resources/vendor-questions/ · Content last reviewed November 2026

Get the answers written into the contract. A demo or a sales page is not enough. Involve whoever handles IT security and ethics or risk questions.

Your data

  • Do you train your models, or anyone else’s, on what we enter or upload?
  • How long do you keep our inputs and outputs? Can we choose zero retention?
  • Where is our data stored, and in which countries?
  • Which of your staff and which other companies can see our data, and why?
  • How do you respond to a subpoena or government request for our data?

Security

  • Which independent security audits or certifications do you hold?
  • How will you tell us about a data breach, and how quickly?
  • Can we sign in with our own accounts and turn on two-step sign-in?

Accuracy

  • How do you measure accuracy, on what kinds of tasks, and can we see the results?
  • How often are citations wrong or unsupported in your testing?
  • Which AI model does the product use, and how will we know when it changes?
  • Can we run a trial on our own test tasks before we buy?

Legal sources

  • Where does the legal content come from, and how current is it?
  • Does every answer link to the source it relied on?

Control

  • Can we limit who sees which matters, so ethical walls hold?
  • Is there a record of who used the tool, when and on what?
  • Can we export and delete all of our data when we leave?

Cost

  • How is it priced, and what costs extra?
  • How will we explain any AI charges to clients?
