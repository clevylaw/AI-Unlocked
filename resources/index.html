Resources

Printable checklists and templates for using AI in legal work.

  • AI use checklistA one-page check to run before, during and after you use AI on legal work.
  • Citation checking worksheetA printable worksheet for checking every authority in a document, whether or not AI helped write it.
  • Questions to ask an AI vendorQuestions to ask before a firm, court, school or legal department adopts an AI tool.
  • Sample AI policy for a legal teamA short starting point for a law firm or legal department policy on generative AI. Adapt it to your own rules.
  • Bar guidance on AIEthics opinions and guidance on lawyers’ use of generative AI from the ABA and several state bars, with links to the originals.

Each resource has a Print button and prints cleanly on one or two pages. They are general education, not legal advice. Adapt them to your own rules.
