AI in everyday life

AI stands for artificial intelligence. You probably use it every day without noticing.

You already use AI

When your phone suggests the next word as you type, that is AI. When a streaming service suggests a show, that is AI too. So is the filter that moves junk email out of your inbox.

All of these do the same kind of job. A computer makes a prediction, which is a careful guess. It bases that guess on patterns it found in lots of examples.

Key idea

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a computer system that does jobs that usually need a person's thinking. Spotting faces, understanding speech and making predictions are all examples.

What AI is not

Films often show AI as a robot that thinks like a person. Real AI has no feelings or opinions, and it does not know that it exists. It is a machine that is good at finding patterns. That makes it useful, and it also makes it different from a human mind.

Example

A junk-mail filter does not "know" that an email is a scam. It has seen millions of emails. It learned which words often show up in scams, and it looks for those words.

Why AI is suddenly everywhere

People have been building AI since the 1950s. It got much better in the last 15 years.

One reason is that the internet gave computers billions of pages and photos to learn from. Computer chips also became much faster, so they can work through all that data. Researchers found better ways for computers to learn from examples, too. The most important of these is called deep learning. You will meet it in the lesson Types of AI.

For technical readers (optional)

Most AI today is machine learning. A machine learning model is a math formula with millions or even trillions of settings, called parameters. Training adjusts those settings until the formula fits the examples. A junk-mail filter is a classifier, which means it sorts things into groups. It learns from emails that people have already marked as junk or not junk. This is called supervised learning. For each new email, it gives a score for how likely the email is to be junk. The reasons for AI's growth in this lesson match what engineers call data scale, special chips (GPUs and TPUs), and better training methods such as backpropagation and the Transformer.
