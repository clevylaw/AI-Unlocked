When chatbots get it wrong

Chatbots can say things that are completely wrong and still sound sure of themselves. This lesson shows what to watch for.

What a "hallucination" is

When an AI states something false as if it were a fact, people call it a hallucination. It might invent a book, get a date wrong or make up a quote. This happens because the AI writes the words that sound most likely, and sometimes the most likely-sounding words are wrong.

Common examples

Made-up sources, such as "A 2019 Harvard study found..." when there was no such study. Wrong dates, such as "The Eiffel Tower was finished in 1854" when it was finished in 1889. Quotes from real people who never said them.

Hallucinations happen less often as models improve. Chatbots that search the web and show their sources are also easier to check. But hallucinations still happen.

How to protect yourself

  • Check anything important with a source you trust. This matters most for health, legal and money questions.
  • Take extra care with details. Numbers, names, dates and sources are where mistakes hide.
  • Click the source links when a chatbot gives them. Make sure each page says what the AI claims.
  • Use AI for drafts and outlines. Then fill in facts you have checked yourself.

A good rule

Treat a chatbot like a coworker who has read a lot but sometimes gets details wrong. It is great for ideas and drafts. Double-check anything important before you send it or rely on it.

For technical readers (optional)

Hallucinations happen because training rewards text that sounds likely, and sounding likely is different from being checked and true. Models are also often poor at judging how unsure they are. Some fixes help. One is to base answers on documents the system looks up, with links to the sources. Another is to tell the model plainly that "I don't know" is an allowed answer. A third is to give it tools for exact jobs, such as search, a calculator or running code. Teams also test models against answers they already know are correct. When you give a chatbot your own sources, ask it to quote them word for word, then check the quotes.
