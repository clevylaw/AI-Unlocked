AI for writing

AI can help you write faster and more clearly. The ideas should still be yours.

Get past the blank page

The hardest part of writing is often getting started. AI is good at writing a first draft that you can then change. It is also good at editing, reordering and changing the tone of your writing.

Ways to use it

  • First drafts. Describe what you need and let AI write a draft. Then rewrite it in your own words.
  • Editing. Paste in your draft and ask the AI to "make this shorter," "fix the grammar" or "make it sound more professional."
  • Tone. Ask it to "make this warmer and less formal" or "make this more direct."
  • Emails. List the points you need to make, and let AI turn them into a clear message.

A good writing prompt

"I need to write a yearly review for someone on my team. She did great work this year, but she often misses deadlines. I want to be honest and encouraging. My notes are in brackets: [your notes]. Write three short paragraphs in a warm, direct tone."

Keep your own voice

The most common mistake is sending AI writing without changing it. It often sounds bland, like it could be about anyone. Rewrite the sentences in your own words. Add examples that only you know. Cut anything that does not sound like you.

For technical readers (optional)

To get the same style across many documents, put your style guide and a few approved examples in the system prompt. Ask for the answer in a fixed structure that a program can check, such as JSON with a title, summary and body. A lower temperature setting makes the wording more consistent from one try to the next. To match a company's voice across many documents, it usually works better to have the system look up past approved writing than to retrain the model, which is called fine-tuning.

Try it yourself

Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and paste in this prompt.

Turn these notes into a short, friendly email to my neighbor: I borrowed their ladder last week. I'll bring it back on Saturday morning. Thank you. I'd be happy to help with their garden fence.
