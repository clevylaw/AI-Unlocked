AI at your legal job

Firms, courts and legal departments are adopting AI. This lesson shows common uses and how to stay within the rules.

Common uses in legal work

  • Summaries. Turn a long deposition, contract or email chain into the main points, then check them against the source.
  • Timelines. Pull dates and events from documents into a table.
  • First-pass review. Flag clauses in a stack of contracts, such as change-of-control or auto-renewal terms.
  • Drafting from templates. Fill in routine documents from your firm's approved forms.
  • Getting ready for meetings. List questions to ask a client or witness.

Important

Use only the AI tools your employer has approved for client work. Never put client names, privileged documents or confidential facts into a public chatbot. If you are not sure whether a tool is approved, ask before you use it.

Billing and honesty

AI can make some tasks much faster. ABA Formal Opinion 512, issued in July 2024, says lawyers who bill by the hour may only bill for the time they actually spent. Lawyers should not charge clients for time spent learning a general AI tool. Firms should tell clients how AI costs will be billed.

Supervising others

Partners and supervising lawyers must make sure the people they supervise use AI properly. That includes associates, paralegals, staff and outside vendors. That means clear policies, training and checking the work.

Staying valuable

AI handles more of the routine drafting and review. Judgment, client counseling, negotiation, advocacy and deep knowledge of an area of law become more important. People who learn to use AI well and check it carefully will be in demand.

For technical readers (optional)

Enterprise AI plans usually add protections such as a promise not to train on your data, short or zero data retention, single sign-on, audit logs and admin controls. If your organization connects AI to its document system, the connection should respect existing access rights, so the AI only shows each person the matters they are allowed to see. Ethical walls between matters must still work. Keep a record of how AI was used on a matter, in case a client or court asks.

Try it yourself

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot and paste in this prompt.
